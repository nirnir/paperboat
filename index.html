About

Senior product judgement, without the permanent hire.

Paper Boat is a product and strategy consultancy built on years spent between strategy and delivery — inside enterprise organisations and fast-moving product teams across Europe, Israel and the US.

Much of our work sits where product strategy meets AI transformation: helping organisations decide what to build, restructuring how product and commercial teams work together, and turning agentic AI from a slide into a working part of the operation.

We engage fractionally. That means embedded enough to be accountable for outcomes, light enough to stay honest about what's actually working — and a deliberate handover to the people who own it once we step back.

Founded by Nir Ehrlich, product executive and fractional CPO.

How we work

Diagnose, decide, deliver.

  1. Diagnose

    A short, intense look at the product, the customers and how the team actually operates. You get a written read of what's really blocking you — not a deck of generic advice.

  2. Decide

    We agree the smallest set of changes that move the needle, with clear owners and a sequence. A strategy that fits on one page beats one nobody remembers.

  3. Deliver

    We stay embedded through execution — running the cadence, unblocking decisions and coaching the people who will own it after we step back.
