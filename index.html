About

Paper Boat is the consulting practice of Nir Ehrlich. I've spent my career between strategy and delivery — in enterprise organisations and in fast-moving product teams across Europe, Israel and the US.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of product strategy and AI transformation: helping organisations decide what to build, restructure how their product and commercial teams work together, and turn agentic AI from a slide into a working part of the operation.

I engage fractionally. That means senior product ownership without a permanent executive hire — embedded enough to be accountable, light enough to stay honest about what's working.

Services

Three ways clients usually bring me in.

Fractional CPO

Product leadership on a part-time basis. Strategy, roadmap and prioritisation, product operating model, hiring and coaching product managers, and acting as the product voice in the leadership team.

  • Product strategy & roadmap
  • Target operating model & ways of working
  • Discovery, personas & user research
  • Pricing & packaging
  • PM coaching & leadership development

Account & client management

Getting complex client relationships back on track. I work on the commercial side of delivery — expectations, governance, escalations — and on the go-to-market motion that keeps accounts growing.

  • Key account strategy & growth
  • Stakeholder alignment & governance
  • Go-to-market & product marketing
  • Delivery & programme management
  • Recovering at-risk accounts

Strategic advisory

Short, focused engagements for a specific decision: an AI adoption plan, a reorganisation, a build-vs-buy call, or a strategy that needs pressure-testing before it goes to the board.

  • AI & agentic enablement roadmaps
  • Strategic planning & option framing
  • Change management
  • Organisation & team design
  • Board and exec-level advisory

How I work

  1. Diagnose

    A short, intense look at the product, the customers and the way the team actually operates. You get a written read of what's really blocking you — not a deck of generic advice.

  2. Decide

    We agree the smallest set of changes that move the needle, with clear owners and a sequence. Strategy that fits on a page beats strategy nobody remembers.

  3. Deliver

    I stay embedded through execution — running the cadence, unblocking decisions and coaching the people who will own it after I leave.
